A self-hostable donation platform for creators, giving them a Ko-fi/Patreon-style experience while keeping full ownership of their data and branding. Supports one-time and recurring donations with a creator dashboard for tracking supporters and analytics.

**Built with:** SvelteKit, Tailwind CSS, Paystack, Airtable
